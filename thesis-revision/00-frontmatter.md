# 中文摘要

鞅 Hardy–Orlicz 空间把经典鞅 Hardy 空间中的平方函数方法与 Orlicz 空间的一般增长条件结合起来。本文研究由平方函数和条件平方函数生成的鞅 Hardy–Orlicz 空间的对偶表示，重点考察幂型复合结构

\[
\Phi(t)=\Phi_1(t^r),\qquad r\ge2
\]

与二次平方函数之间的相容性。

对普通平方函数

\[
S(X)=\left(\sum_{n\ge1}|dX_n|^2\right)^{1/2},
\]

本文引入辅助 Young 函数

\[
A_\Phi(u)=\Phi(\sqrt u)=\Phi_1(u^{r/2}).
\]

当 \(\Phi\) 的 Luxemburg 范数具有序连续性，且相应条件期望凸性不等式成立时，本文证明相关投影在向量值 Orlicz 空间上有界。结合 Hahn–Banach 定理和向量值 Orlicz 对偶，得到

\[
(H_S^\Phi)^*\simeq H_S^\Psi,
\]

其中 \(\Psi\) 为 \(\Phi\) 的共轭 Young 函数。本文还研究条件平方函数

\[
s(X)=\left(\sum_{n\ge1}E_{n-1}|dX_n|^2\right)^{1/2}
\]

生成的空间，并在对应假设下得到

\[
(H_s^\Phi)^*\simeq H_s^\Psi.
\]

在条件尾量表示方面，本文先通过 Garsia 型条件尾量假设构造 \(\widetilde K^\Psi\) 代表。在进一步假设

\[
\Psi(t)=\Psi_1(t^s),\qquad s\ge2,
\]

且 Doob 极大算子在 \(L^{\Psi_1}\) 上有界时，把二阶条件尾量控制转化为一阶控制。在 Fefferman–Garsia 型配对不等式成立时，得到

\[
(H_S^\Phi)^*\simeq K^\Psi.
\]

本文明确区分 Hardy 型对偶与 \(K\) 型对偶所需的不同假设，并补充有限鞅稠密性、Orlicz 范数序连续性、无穷配对绝对收敛、中心化截断及代表元唯一性等环节。本文的幂型复合条件主要适用于至少具有二次下增长的 Young 函数，不直接覆盖 \(H^1\) 端点和 \(1<p<2\) 的经典 \(H^p\) 空间。

**关键词：** 鞅 Hardy–Orlicz 空间；Young 函数；对偶空间；平方函数；条件平方函数；\(K^\Psi\) 空间

# Abstract

Martingale Hardy–Orlicz spaces combine square-function methods from martingale Hardy space theory with the general growth structure of Orlicz spaces. This thesis studies dual representations of spaces generated by the ordinary and conditional square functions, with emphasis on the compatibility between

\[
\Phi(t)=\Phi_1(t^r),\qquad r\ge2,
\]

and the quadratic structure of martingale square functions.

For

\[
S(X)=\left(\sum_{n\ge1}|dX_n|^2\right)^{1/2},
\]

we introduce

\[
A_\Phi(u)=\Phi(\sqrt u)=\Phi_1(u^{r/2}).
\]

Under order continuity of the Luxemburg norm and the relevant conditional-expectation convexity inequalities, the associated projections are bounded on vector-valued Orlicz spaces. Hahn–Banach extension and vector-valued Orlicz duality then yield

\[
(H_S^\Phi)^*\simeq H_S^\Psi.
\]

The conditional-square-function counterpart is also considered, and under the corresponding assumptions we obtain

\[
(H_s^\Phi)^*\simeq H_s^\Psi.
\]

For conditional-tail representations, a representing element in \(\widetilde K^\Psi\) is first constructed under a Garsia-type tail assumption. If

\[
\Psi(t)=\Psi_1(t^s),\qquad s\ge2,
\]

and the Doob maximal operator is bounded on \(L^{\Psi_1}\), second-order conditional-tail control can be converted into first-order control. Together with an appropriate Fefferman–Garsia pairing inequality, this gives

\[
(H_S^\Phi)^*\simeq K^\Psi.
\]

The assumptions for Hardy-type and \(K\)-type dualities are stated separately. Particular attention is paid to finite-martingale density, order continuity, absolute convergence of the pairing, centered truncations, and uniqueness of representing elements. The power-composition framework considered here applies mainly to Young functions with at least quadratic lower growth and does not directly include \(H^1\) or the classical range \(1<p<2\).

**Keywords:** martingale Hardy–Orlicz space; Young function; dual space; square function; conditional square function; \(K^\Psi\) space
