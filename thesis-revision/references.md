15. Dam B K. The dual of the martingale Hardy space \(H_\Phi\) with general Young function \(\Phi\). *Analysis Mathematica*, 1988, 14(4): 287–294. DOI: 10.1007/BF02074613.
16. Dam B K. Connection between the BMO- and the \(K_\Phi\)-spaces. *Annales Universitatis Scientiarum Budapestinensis, Sectio Computatorica*, 1988, 9: 51–66.
17. Bassily N L, Abdel-Fattah A M. On the duality of some martingale spaces. *Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society*, 1992, 45(1): 43–52. DOI: 10.1017/S0004972700036996.
18. Dam B K. General form of linear functionals on Hardy spaces. *Acta Mathematica Vietnamica*, 2003, 28(3): 259–266.

> 文献定位：Dam 1988 和 Bassily–Abdel-Fattah 1992 已讨论一般 Young 函数或双方有限幂下的对偶。正文不得把这些文献简单描述为只含二次复合情形。Dam 2003 是否具有特定二次复合假设须从全文逐字核对。
